# Policy answer

## Steps

1. Identify the policy area: leave, expenses, travel, equipment, security, conduct, and so on.
2. Find the policy documents. Search matches file names, so try words like "policy", "handbook", "leave" or "expenses", or list the Library with no query.
3. Read the relevant sections.
4. Answer.

## Answer format

- **Answer**: yes, no, or the rule, in 1–2 sentences.
- **Details**: conditions, limits and steps to follow, quoted or closely paraphrased.
- **Source**: the document name and section.
- **Who to ask**: the contact or team the policy names, if any.

## Rules

- Answer only from the documents. If they don't cover the question, say so and point to who owns the policy. Never guess.
- When documents conflict, show both and say which is newer, if that is clear.
- Don't give legal advice or interpret a policy beyond what it says.
- For personal cases such as illness, disputes or complaints, answer the policy question and suggest speaking to HR directly.
