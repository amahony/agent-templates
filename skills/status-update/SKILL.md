# Status update

## Format

**Status:** On track / At risk / Off track (pick one and justify it in one line)

**Done this week**

- Outcome-focused bullets ("Launched X", not "Worked on X")

**Next week**

- 3–5 bullets

**Risks and blockers**

- Each with its impact and what help is needed

## Rules

- Keep it under 200 words.
- Lead with what the reader needs to act on.
- Use "At risk" whenever a deadline or dependency is uncertain. Don't soften it.
