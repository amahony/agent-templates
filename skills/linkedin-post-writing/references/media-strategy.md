# Media strategy

On mobile the image takes about 80% of the screen and the hook about 20%.
Treat the image as the post's cover. Describe the image to make; you cannot
create it.

## What works

- **Format:** a single portrait image, 1080 × 1350 px, which fills the feed.
- **Style:** scrappy and unbranded, like a whiteboard sketch. The more it looks
  like an ad, the worse it does.
- **Content:** useful on its own, something a reader would save or send to a
  colleague: a cheat sheet, a framework, a before-and-after or an annotated
  screenshot.

## What does not

- Polished corporate graphics with brand colours and logos
- Stock photos
- Selfies, unless the author is already well known
- Videos
- Carousels longer than five slides

## Suggesting how to make it

When the author wants to make the image with an AI image tool, suggest:

1. Find a reference for the structure, for example on Pinterest:
   "[field] infographic", "cheat sheet" or "graph".
2. Find a second reference for the style: hand-drawn, sketched or organic.
3. Combine the structure of the first with the style of the second.
