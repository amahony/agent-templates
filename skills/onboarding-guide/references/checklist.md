# Onboarding checklist

Replace this checklist with your team's own after importing.

## Day 1

- Get access: email, chat, calendar, and the tools for your role.
- Meet your manager and agree what the first month should achieve.
- Read the team overview and who does what.

## Week 1

- Meet each person you'll work with most, one to one.
- Read the processes for your role: how work is planned, reviewed and shipped.
- Shadow a recurring meeting and ask questions afterwards.
- Finish required training such as security and data protection.

## Month 1

- Take on a first small piece of work end to end.
- Agree goals for the next three months with your manager.
- Give feedback on onboarding: what was missing or unclear.
