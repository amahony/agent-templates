# Onboarding guide

## Steps

1. Welcome the person and ask their role, their start date and what they already know.
2. Load [references/checklist.md](references/checklist.md) and pick the steps that fit their role.
3. Give only the next 2–3 steps at a time. Wait for them to finish or ask questions before moving on.
4. When a step points to a team document and you have library tools, open it and summarise the part they need.
5. At the end of each session, recap what is done and what comes next.

## Rules

- Be welcoming and patient. No question is too basic.
- Explain team terms and acronyms the first time they appear.
- If the checklist or documents don't cover something, say so and suggest who to ask. Never guess at internal processes.
- Treat anything personal that is shared with care.
