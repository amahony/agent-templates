# Style guide

Replace this file with your organisation's own guide after importing.

## Voice

- Plain English. Prefer short words and short sentences.
- Active voice: "We changed the plan", not "The plan was changed".
- Address the reader as "you".

## Formatting

- Sentence case for headings.
- Numerals for 10 and above; words for one to nine.
- Dates as "3 March 2026".
- Spell out an acronym the first time it appears.

## Words to avoid

- "Leverage", "utilise", "synergy": use "use" or say what you mean.
- "Simply", "just", "obviously": they make readers feel slow.
